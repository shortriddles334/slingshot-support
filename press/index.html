Short description

Every planet you catch plays a note. Every run draws a new constellation. Fly the Daily Orbit: the same sky for everyone, a different song for you.

Description

Tap to let go. Tap to catch. Swing your comet from planet to planet, and every planet you catch plays the next note of your run's melody.

Dim planets wake as you catch them, and your flight threads them into a constellation. When the run ends, the camera pulls back to reveal the sky you drew, named just for you.

The complete game is free. An optional Supporter Pack adds three animated trails, a gold comet halo and a Supporter badge. No ads, no energy timers, no paid advantages.

Key features

  • One-tap play: tap to release from orbit, catch the next planet's ring, stay ahead of the rising void.
  • Every catch plays the next note of your run's melody.
  • Each run draws a constellation, revealed and named for you at the end.
  • Chain catches into combos, hit x12 for Overdrive, and skim planets for CLOSE! bonuses.
  • Cross Warp Gates into the Rocky Belt, Frost Ring and Gas Giants.
  • Shield, Slow-Time and Magnet pickups.
  • Daily Orbit: one seeded sky per day, streaks and a spoiler-free share card.
  • Missions, ranks and a Hangar of cosmetics earned by playing. Cosmetics never change stats.
  • Haptics and color-blind-safe rings.
